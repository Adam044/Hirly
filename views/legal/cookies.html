Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help us remember your preferences and provide a more personalized experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: Necessary for the website to function, such as authentication and security.
  • Functional: Remembering your language preferences and UI settings.
  • Analytics: Understanding how users interact with Hirly to improve our features.
  • Marketing: Serving relevant job opportunities and career insights.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of the platform.
